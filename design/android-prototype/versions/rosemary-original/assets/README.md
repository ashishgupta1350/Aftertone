## Dreamscape

- File: `dreamscape.png`
- Method: built-in imagegen tool, 19 November 2026.
- Purpose: local album-cover artwork in the Android prototype.
- Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square album artwork for an Android music player prototype. Create a cinematic dreamy photograph of a lone small dark silhouette seated on a rocky coastal lookout, seen from behind, looking across a calm Mediterranean bay at dusk. Curving coastline and distant tiny warm town lights on the right, layered mountains on horizon. Big luminous pink and peach sunset near horizon, violet clouds and deep lavender sky above, purple ocean reflecting dusk. Composition: square, silhouette small in lower center, strong atmospheric depth, beautiful realistic landscape details, quiet introspective music mood. Rich lilac, dusty pink, periwinkle, warm peach highlights and deep indigo shadows, inspired by the attached third reference's album cover only. Full bleed landscape photograph. No text, no lettering, no borders, no icons, no music player controls, no interface, no watermark. Output only the landscape cover art.
